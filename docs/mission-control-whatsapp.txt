*Wild Drone Appeared — UI TL;DR*

Our project coordinates 3 battery-limited drones and 1 shared charger to keep important wildfire locations recently inspected. The full fire map is visible; the drones inspect rather than extinguish it.

*The UI shows* movement/trails, fire and fuel, inspection freshness, batteries, charging and decisions. We can pause, scrub through steps and compare controllers. The research/results pages explain how we got here and what the experiments found.

*Animation:* Python saves the actual state after each simulator step; Canvas draws it and smooths drone movement. A complete 300-step mission has 301 snapshots. Playback speed changes viewing speed, not physics or results.

*Two modes:*
• Showcase: 39 verified archived replays; aggregate charts use all 260 main-study missions.
• Local lab: new Python simulations with adjustable conditions. Live RL needs verified checkpoints. No new training is launched.

*Main finding:* The mask helped safety, but reliable learned inspection improvement was not demonstrated. Greedy freshness was 22.08%, versus DQN 8.44% and masked random 8.38%. Freshness measures recency, not land coverage.
