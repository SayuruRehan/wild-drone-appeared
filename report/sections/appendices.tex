\section{Questions for independent oral defence}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\item
  Which fields make the environment fully observable, and why is the task not unknown-fire discovery?
\item
  How do 5$^3$ joint actions encode movement and how do collision/charge rules constrain their effects?
\item
  Can you derive the exact reward terms from the implementation and explain early-failure accounting?
\item
  What distinguishes DQN's TD target, REINFORCE's full Monte Carlo return, and A2C's n-step bootstrap?
\item
  Why does zero drone loss not imply learning or successful charging coordination?
\item
  How is seed-level uncertainty calculated and why are 60 test missions not 60 independent fitted models?
\item
  Which 20$\times$20 differences make the apparent scaling trend noncausal?
\item
  What evidence justifies each proposed improvement and what new evaluation would disprove it?
\item
  How would a partial-observation extension alter S, O, P and the policy input?
\item
  Which contribution percentages and notebook/report authorship have been agreed by the group?
\end{enumerate}
